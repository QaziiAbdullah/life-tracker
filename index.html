How Goals Work — Simple!

Daily Goals — Things you want to do every day. Tick them off each morning/evening. Like habits but goal-focused.
Weekly Goals — Things you want to do once a week. Tick it when done this week.
Monthly / Yearly Goals — Big targets with a number. Click +1 each time you make progress (e.g. finish a book = +1).

Daily Goals — Tick Every Day
Weekly Goals — Tick Once This Week
Monthly Goals — Click +1 for Each Progress
